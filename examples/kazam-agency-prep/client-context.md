# Client Context — Kazam Energy (Agency Prep Example)

> Last updated: 2026-08-19
> Mode: public-source prospect research
> Purpose: demonstrate agency GTM intelligence, not internal company diagnosis

## Company snapshot

- **Company:** Kazam EV Tech / Kazam Energy
- **Website:** https://kazam.energy/
- **Category:** EV charging infrastructure software, hardware and energy-management technology
- **Founded:** 2020
- **Scale context:** Economic Times reported FY25 revenue around ₹40 crore; management was targeting roughly ₹85–100 crore / ₹100-crore run-rate in FY26.
- **Geography:** India with international presence in SEA, MENA and North America according to Kazam.

## What Kazam sells

Kazam presents a broad e-mobility/energy stack spanning:

- EV charge points / hardware
- charging management system (CMS)
- charging billing and revenue management
- operations & maintenance / charger health
- energy management
- vehicle/depot management
- EV leasing management
- battery swapping management
- roaming / interoperability
- white-label solutions

## Public buyer/use-case groups

Kazam currently speaks to many audiences including:

- Charge Point Operators
- OEMs
- e-commerce / quick-commerce fleets
- fleet operators
- utilities
- EV leasing businesses
- commercial buildings
- RWAs
- construction companies
- petrol pumps
- homes / residential charging

## Current visible positioning

Kazam's homepage currently positions the company as a **"Global Technology Enabler for e-Mobility & Energy Management"** and emphasizes a large integrated technology stack rather than a single charging-network proposition.

Visible proof includes charger/session/user/energy metrics and compatibility with many charger OEMs.

## Public growth signals

- FY25 revenue reported around ₹40 crore.
- Management expected significant FY26 growth, with tier-2/3 cities contributing to expansion.
- Kazam has stated significant strength in electric three-wheelers and plans to expand further into two-wheelers and e-buses.
- Kazam says it has worked with OEMs such as Bajaj, Mahindra, Ather and TVS and with fleet/CPO/energy partners including HPCL.
- Kazam's 2025 funding announcement highlighted 68,000+ deployed/managed chargers at that time, 45 GWh delivered and operations across 10 countries; current website metrics are larger and should be treated as current company claims.

## Visible GTM assets

### Website / conversion

- broad solution navigation by buyer and product
- `Get in touch`, `Talk to Expert`, demo/contact-style CTAs
- partner/dealer intake page
- product pages with quantified use-case outcomes

### Proof / case-study style

Kazam's billing/CMS pages show quantified examples such as:

- 1,000+ site oil & gas deployment
- reduced onboarding/go-live time
- field-visit reduction
- time-of-day tariff experiments
- fleet-specific tariff automation

Several examples are anonymized rather than customer-named.

### Partnerships / ecosystem narrative

Kazam repeatedly uses OEM, fleet, fuel-retail and interoperability partnerships as credibility and distribution signals.

### Content / category narrative

Kazam publishes company news and broader EV-industry update content, including partnership and ecosystem developments.
