## 1. Reconciled account story

Kazam should not be treated as a single enterprise-software GTM story.

The verified public evidence points to **two different commercial engines** that an agency must distinguish before proposing work.

### Core revenue engine — light EV / home charging / tier-2/3

Current management interviews say tier-2/3 cities are Kazam's biggest revenue generator, home charging for three-wheelers is its biggest revenue driver, and three-wheelers account for a major share of current top-line growth. Kazam's own company narrative also describes home charging and OEM relationships across two- and three-wheelers as foundational to its growth.

This means an agency that walks in with only an enterprise-CMS/ABM pitch risks ignoring the business that currently appears to pay the bills.

### Expansion engine — fleet, CPO/public charging, software/energy and adjacent enterprise use cases

Kazam also has a broad enterprise technology stack spanning CMS, energy management, fleet/leasing, battery swapping, O&M, roaming and other use cases. Current product pages show significant operating proof and multiple enterprise buyer types.

The agency therefore needs a **two-engine GTM view**:

1. defend/scale the established 2W/3W home-charging and regional/OEM ecosystem;
2. build sharper proof and buyer journeys for expansion businesses where sales cycles and trust requirements are different.

## 2. Verified competitor GTM patterns

### Pattern A — partner/channel acquisition is widespread, but importance is unproven

Verified public paths exist across the competitor set:

- Statiq: FOCO/franchise and property/commercial partner acquisition
- ChargeZone: financed franchise proposition
- Bolt.Earth: distributor/channel partner acquisition
- Kazam: partner/dealer intake

**Verified conclusion:** partner/site/channel acquisition is a recurring visible GTM motion in this category.

**Not verified:** that this is a major revenue source for Kazam or every competitor.

**Agency implication:** audit partner economics and priority before pitching a large partner-acquisition program.

Sources:
- https://www.statiq.in/partner-with-us
- https://www.chargezone.co.in/franchise
- https://bolt.earth/become-a-distributor
- https://kazam.energy/become-a-partner

### Pattern B — named proof is a strong enterprise selling convention

Bolt.Earth has a visible named case-study library including Optum, Bank of America and SAP. Kazam has meaningful quantified outcome examples on current product pages, but several are labelled by customer archetype (for example an Oil & Gas Major, CPO or Quick-Commerce Fleet) rather than company name.

**Agency implication:** investigate a proof architecture program—named where permission exists, quantified/anonymized where it does not. The goal is buyer-specific credibility, not logos for decoration.

Sources:
- https://bolt.earth/resources/case-studies
- https://kazam.energy/products/ev-charging-billing-software
- https://kazam.energy/products/energy-management

### Pattern C — buyer/use-case segmentation is common, but Kazam is not starting from zero

Bolt.Earth has dedicated workplace, enterprise, RWA, parking and builder surfaces. Statiq separates franchise, property and fleet use cases. Kazam itself already exposes multiple audience and product-specific pages.

**Agency implication:** do not diagnose “bad information architecture” from the outside. First examine analytics, pipeline source and sales feedback. A simplification/prioritization opportunity may exist, but its commercial impact is currently an inference.

## 3. Corrected research/category-authority thesis

The original research said proprietary-data/category authority was whitespace. Verification disproved that.

Kazam already runs a research motion. Its EV Ready Homes initiative says it uses insights from 100,000+ home charger installations and points to a report with the Alliance for an Energy Efficient Economy; Business Standard reported that joint home-charging study in June 2026. Competitors such as Bolt.Earth also publish data-rich category explainers.

**Agency opportunity is therefore not “start publishing research.”**

A better hypothesis is:

> Turn Kazam's existing research/data capability into a repeatable GTM asset that connects research → PR → search → executive narrative → partner/OEM education → sales collateral.

Whether that deserves agency investment depends on existing results and available publishable data.

## 5. Agency directions worth investigating

### Direction 1 — Core-engine regional/OEM GTM audit

**Why now:** independent evidence says three-wheelers, home charging and tier-2/3 markets are economically important today.

**What the agency should investigate:**

- how an OEM/customer moves from vehicle sale to charger installation/use
- dealer/install partner role
- city/PIN-code differences
- safety/education friction
- referral/word-of-mouth opportunities
- regional search/discovery
- service/proof assets

**Do not assume:** paid media is the answer. First understand the existing OEM/dealer/channel journey.

**Confidence:** high that this deserves discovery; execution choice unknown.

### Direction 2 — Enterprise proof system

**Why now:** Bolt visibly packages named enterprise case studies; Kazam already has strong quantified outcome examples.

**Agency hypothesis:** create a repeatable proof system by buyer type—case studies, proof blocks, sales one-pagers and implementation stories—using named customers only where permissions allow.

**Confidence:** high.

### Direction 3 — Research-to-revenue distribution system

**Why now:** Kazam already has a data/research capability rather than a blank slate.

**Agency hypothesis:** audit how current research is distributed and used; then build a recurring workflow that turns strong research into search assets, PR, founder/executive narrative, partner education and sales collateral.

**Confidence:** medium-high; depends on current performance and publishable data.

### Direction 4 — Buyer-journey simplification audit for expansion products

**Why now:** Kazam's portfolio spans many products/audiences while competitors use explicit buyer/use-case pages.

**Agency hypothesis:** sales/pipeline data may reveal places where Kazam's breadth creates unnecessary complexity.

**Do not pitch:** a website rebuild before proving the bottleneck.

**Confidence:** medium.

### Direction 5 — Partner acquisition only if economics justify it

**Why now:** partner programs are visible across Kazam and competitors.

**Agency hypothesis:** one or more partner types may merit a dedicated acquisition/enablement journey.

**Do not pitch:** franchise/dealer campaigns merely because competitors have them.

**Confidence:** low-medium until client data confirms importance.

## 6. What the agency should NOT pitch from public research alone

- an enterprise-only repositioning
- a full website rebuild
- “launch an industry index” as if Kazam has no research capability
- a franchise program
- mass partner lead generation
- generic SEO/social/paid-media retainers
- a tier-2/3 budget shift without pipeline/channel data

## 7. Discovery questions that now matter most

1. What is the FY26/FY27 revenue and gross-margin mix across 2W/3W home charging, fleets, public/CPO, software/energy, leasing and other products?
2. Which segment management most wants to accelerate over the next 12 months: the current 3W engine or newer enterprise products?
3. How does a three-wheeler customer actually reach Kazam—OEM, dealer, installer, direct search, referral or another route?
4. Which tier-2/3 markets are profitable/growing versus simply large deployment bases?
5. What percentage of enterprise pipeline comes inbound, partner-led, founder-led, OEM-led, channel-led or event-led?
6. Which competitor appears most often in real deals?
7. Which customer names and outcomes can be used publicly versus only in gated sales collateral?
8. Do current research reports create measurable press, traffic, leads, partner conversations or sales usage?
9. Which partner types generate real deployments/revenue, and which are exploratory?
10. Where does the sales team currently lose time explaining the product/portfolio?

## 8. Final agency pitch inputs

- **One-line account opportunity:** Help Kazam market two different growth engines deliberately: scale its proven regional/3W home-charging ecosystem while sharpening proof and buyer journeys for enterprise expansion.
- **Top evidence 1:** current management commentary says tier-2/3 and three-wheeler home charging are major revenue drivers.
- **Top evidence 2:** competitors visibly use partner/channel acquisition and segmented buyer journeys, but public data does not prove those motions should be copied wholesale.
- **Top evidence 3:** Bolt packages named enterprise proof strongly; Kazam already has quantified proof that could be organized more systematically.
- **Research correction:** Kazam already has a data-led research motion; the opportunity is distribution/commercialization, not invention.
- **Best competitor benchmark for enterprise proof:** Bolt.Earth.
- **Partner benchmark to study, not automatically copy:** Statiq.
- **Biggest unknown:** where marketing actually constrains Kazam's two GTM engines.
