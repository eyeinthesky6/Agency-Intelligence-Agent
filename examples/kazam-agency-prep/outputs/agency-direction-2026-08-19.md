# Agency Direction Brief — Kazam Energy

> Date: 2026-08-19
> Audience: agency founder / strategist preparing a pitch
> This is prep material, not a client proposal

## 1. The account story

Kazam appears to be moving from an EV-charger startup story toward a broad enterprise **mobility + energy technology stack**. Publicly, it now speaks to OEMs, CPOs, fleets, utilities, leasing businesses, properties and partners while offering hardware, charging management, billing, O&M, depot, energy, swapping and roaming products.

That breadth is strategically useful but creates a marketing challenge: each buyer has a different job, sales trigger and proof requirement.

Competitors show the market becoming more structured around these buyer journeys. Statiq productizes franchise/site ownership and property/fleet deployment. Bolt.Earth creates many use-case-specific search/landing paths and backs enterprise selling with named case studies. ChargeZone clearly separates intercity consumer charging, fleet electrification, franchise/site investment and loyalty.

Kazam's strongest visible advantages are **technology breadth, ecosystem relationships, multi-OEM/device-agnostic capability and quantified operating proof**. The agency opportunity is therefore not to manufacture more generic EV visibility. It is to make those strengths easier for each priority buyer to discover, trust and act on.

## 2. Competitive GTM read

### Table stakes

- large deployment/network/platform proof
- recognizable customer/partner logos
- buyer/use-case pages
- category education
- partnership announcements
- business/enterprise contact paths

### Strong competitor playbooks

**Bolt.Earth — use-case demand capture + named proof**

Best benchmark for organizing a broad charging offer around specific business environments and customer stories.

**Statiq — partner/site acquisition funnel**

Best benchmark for explaining a commercial partner model and turning it into self-qualifying inbound.

**ChargeZone — distinct GTM motions under one brand**

Useful example of separating consumer, fleet, franchise and loyalty journeys without pretending they are the same buyer.

### Potential Kazam white space

**Operational intelligence as marketing.** Kazam has access to charger/session/fleet/energy data that could support category benchmarks and executive content competitors cannot easily imitate.

## 4. Marketing/GTM directions

### Direction 1 — Rebuild the story around buyer jobs

**Audience:** OEM, fleet, CPO, fuel/site network, leasing, property

**Why now:** Kazam's solution breadth is large while competitors increasingly make buyer-specific journeys explicit.

**Evidence:** broad Kazam product/segment navigation + Bolt/Statiq use-case segmentation.

**Possible agency proposal:** buyer-journey architecture across website, proof, search and sales enablement.

**Do not promise:** conversion uplift until existing analytics are reviewed.

**Confidence:** high that segmentation improves clarity; impact magnitude unknown.

### Direction 2 — Build an enterprise proof engine

**Audience:** enterprise prospects and partner decision makers

**Why now:** Kazam already has good quantified examples; competitors such as Bolt package named enterprise cases visibly.

**Evidence:** Kazam CMS/billing outcome examples + Bolt named case-study library.

**Possible agency proposal:** systematic case-study capture, proof blocks, customer-story sales assets and use-case-specific implementation pages.

**Confidence:** high.

### Direction 3 — Turn Kazam data into category authority

**Audience:** enterprise operators, OEMs, utilities, CPOs, policy/ecosystem stakeholders

**Why now:** generic EV news is easy to copy; operational data is less copyable.

**Evidence:** Kazam manages large volumes of chargers/sessions/energy and publicly reports platform metrics.

**Possible agency proposal:** quarterly Kazam EV Infrastructure/Charging Operations Index, benchmark reports, executive commentary and derivative PR/LinkedIn/event material.

**Confidence:** medium-high if publishable data exists.

### Direction 4 — Productize partner acquisition

**Audience:** whichever dealer/site/installer/implementation partner classes management says matter

**Why now:** partner acquisition is visible across all major competitors.

**Evidence:** Kazam partner/dealer intake vs Statiq FOCO, ChargeZone franchise, Bolt distributor programs.

**Possible agency proposal:** partner ICPs, dedicated landing journeys, partner education/proof and regional recruitment.

**Confidence:** medium until partner economics/priority are known.

### Direction 5 — Tier-2/3 regional GTM where evidence justifies it

**Audience:** local partners, properties, fleets, businesses and regional ecosystems

**Why now:** management has publicly cited tier-2/3 growth.

**Evidence:** reported growth narrative + physical/channel nature of charging expansion.

**Possible agency proposal:** identify 5–10 priority regional markets from internal data, then build local proof/search/partner plays rather than 100 generic city pages.

**Confidence:** medium.

## 5. What the agency could pitch

### Pitch module A — Enterprise Demand & Proof System

Why credible:

- Kazam has many enterprise buyer groups
- quantified proof exists
- competitors use clearer segment-specific proof

Scope could include buyer journeys, high-intent pages, proof/case studies, sales assets and selected ABM.

### Pitch module B — Partner / Regional Growth System

Why credible:

- category expansion is partner/site heavy
- Kazam already has partner intake
- tier-2/3 growth is publicly important

Scope could include partner segmentation, recruitment funnels, regional campaigns/search and channel enablement.

### Pitch module C — Kazam Intelligence / Category Authority

Why credible:

- platform sees large operating datasets
- partnerships give ecosystem perspective
- data-led authority is harder to copy than generic EV content

Scope could include proprietary reports, executive narrative, PR, LinkedIn, webinars and sales derivatives.

### Pitch module D — Named-Account GTM

Why credible:

- enterprise customer count can be relatively concentrated/high-value
- relevant buyer universes are identifiable

Scope could include 30–100 target accounts by one segment, account research, proof assets and sales/marketing coordination rather than broad lead volume.

## 6. What NOT to pitch first

- generic social-media management
- "we will grow followers"
- broad SEO without buyer/use-case intent
- mass lead generation without knowing sales priorities/capacity
- consumer influencer work unless consumer/network usage is a strategic priority
- a franchise campaign before confirming Kazam actually wants that business model
- a 12-channel content calendar because competitors publish often

## 7. Questions before proposal

1. Which 3 customer segments matter most to FY27 revenue?
2. What is the pipeline split by segment/product?
3. Where do qualified opportunities come from today?
4. Which competitors show up in actual deals?
5. Which buyer objections delay sales most?
6. Which customer names/results can be public?
7. Which partner classes actually generate revenue or deployment volume?
8. Which tier-2/3 markets are strategic priorities?
9. What marketing/sales work is already owned internally or by agencies?
10. What outcome must improve in 90 days for the agency engagement to be considered successful?

## 8. Agency pitch inputs

- **One-line account opportunity:** Simplify Kazam's broad technology advantage into buyer-specific demand, proof and partner journeys.
- **Top evidence point 1:** Competitors increasingly organize GTM around specific customer/use-case funnels.
- **Top evidence point 2:** Partner/channel/site acquisition is a repeated category growth motion.
- **Top evidence point 3:** Kazam has strong quantified enterprise proof and proprietary operating data that can be marketed more deliberately.
- **Top audiences:** enterprise fleet/OEM/CPO or fuel-network buyers; partner/channel segment as a separate decision.
- **Top GTM directions:** buyer journeys, proof engine, data-led authority, partner/regional growth, named-account GTM.
- **Competitor to benchmark for website/proof:** Bolt.Earth.
- **Competitor to benchmark for partner funnel:** Statiq.
- **Biggest unknown:** which customer/product/partner segments actually drive the highest-quality pipeline and revenue.
