## Company snapshot

- **Website:** https://www.wakefit.co/
- **Category:** mattresses, furniture and home products
- **What they do:** sell mattresses plus a broad home-furnishing range online and through physical stores in India
- **Geography:** India
- **Scale:** Wakefit's site currently states 25 lakh+ customers and 188+ stores

- **Core historic category:** mattresses
- **Broader categories visible publicly:** beds, sofas/seating, wardrobes, tables, bedding, decor and other home categories
- **Primary conversion actions:** ecommerce purchase and store visit
- **Customer assurance signals:** free shipping/installation, warranty messaging and mattress trial messaging are prominent on the public site
