# Client Context — Sintercom India Limited

> Last updated: 2026-08-19
> Context quality: strong public-data prospect context
> Scope: public information only; management verification required for internal commercial conclusions

## Company snapshot

- **Website:** https://sintercom.co.in/
- **Location:** Pune, Maharashtra
- **Category:** powder-metallurgy / sintered precision components
- **FY26 revenue:** approximately ₹100.7 crore
- **FY26 PAT:** approximately ₹1.43 crore
- **Core business:** high-volume, high-precision sintered components for automotive engine, transmission, exhaust/body/chassis and sensor applications
- **Technology relationship:** joint venture / technology relationship with Miba Sinter; 2025 manufacturing licensing arrangement with Horizon Technology for SMC motor components

## Current economic engine

Public disclosures show the company remains predominantly automotive and OEM/Tier-1 led. Historical/current products include:

- engine gears / sprockets / pulleys / bearing caps
- transmission gears / synchronizer hubs / dog rings
- stainless-steel Hego bosses/flanges
- ABS/sensor parts
- drivetrain components

CRISIL reported that the top five customers represented roughly 70–80% of turnover and that the company was a single-source supplier for roughly 80% of products as of FY25.

## Emerging product/market engine

The public product roadmap shows movement beyond legacy ICE applications:

- EV auxiliary components
- helical pulleys for EPS
- precision actuator components
- soft magnetic composite (SMC) components
- SMC stators for electric motors
- axial-flux / EV motor applications
- stainless-steel export programs
- stated ambition to diversify beyond automotive

## Important commercial events

### Tenneco export LOI

Public reporting says Sintercom received an LOI from Tenneco Automotive Operating Co. Inc. and affiliates for Hego Boss/flange products with an estimated three-year contract value of ₹153.2 crore, with supply expected to start around September/October 2025.

This is a very large program relative to a ~₹100.7 crore annual revenue base.

### Horizon / EVR SMC arrangement

A July 2025 disclosure states that Sintercom and Horizon Technology entered a manufacturing licensing arrangement. Under applicable statements of work, Sintercom has exclusive rights to manufacture and supply SMC stator/parts for EVR-designed electric motors for automotive and industrial applications. The arrangement has an initial five-year term. Sintercom subsequently disclosed receipt of a purchase order for SMC stator/parts.

## Financial / operating constraints worth remembering

CRISIL reported as of March 2025:

- gross current assets: ~409 days
- receivables: ~193 days
- inventory: ~235 days
- payables: ~220 days
- bank-limit utilisation: ~95% average over the prior 12 months
- top five customers: ~70–80% of turnover
- all revenue derived from automotive at that time

FY26 public results show revenue of ~₹100.7 crore, PAT of ~₹1.43 crore and finance cost of roughly ₹5.31 crore.

## Competitive frame

### Direct / global PM competitors

- GKN Powder Metallurgy / GKN Sinter Metals
- Porite and other international sintered-component suppliers
- Indian powder-metallurgy specialists

### More important substitutes

The buyer can also choose:

- forging + machining
- casting + machining
- CNC/subtractive manufacturing
- imported finished components
- in-house manufacture
- laminated electrical steel solutions in motor applications, depending on design

The commercial sale is therefore often **manufacturing-process substitution**, not merely supplier substitution.

## Current public positioning

Sintercom currently emphasizes:

- high volume
- high precision
- low cost
- integrated manufacturing
- quality
- powder-metallurgy expertise

This is credible but generic. It understates:

- localization / import substitution
- application co-engineering
- material-yield / machining reduction
- single-source reliability
- EV/SMC access and exclusivity
- export capability
- buyer working economics / total cost

## Agency hypothesis

The best marketing/product work is likely **commercial engineering**, not broad awareness marketing:

1. turn PM process advantages into buyer-specific economic proof
2. package EV/SMC capabilities as a new application-development proposition
3. use technical/application content to enter adjacent non-auto accounts
4. strengthen account expansion and diversification rather than simply add top-of-funnel leads
5. align pricing/terms with working-capital reality on large programs
